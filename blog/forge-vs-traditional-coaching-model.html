The FORGE Model

WHY WE BUILT FORGE DIFFERENTLY THAN THE COACHING PROGRAMS WE USED TO SELL

September 16, 2026 · 6 min read

Before FORGE existed, we sold real estate education the traditional way — from the stage, from the back of the room at live events, inside high-ticket coaching programs. That experience is exactly why FORGE exists, and it's worth being direct about what that old model does well, and where it consistently leaves people stuck.

The Traditional Model Isn't Wrong — It's Incomplete

A great live event or coaching program can genuinely change how someone thinks about real estate in a single weekend. The energy, the community, the immersion — that's real, and we're not here to tell you it doesn't work. The gap isn't in the teaching. It's in what happens on Monday, after the event ends and the room empties out, when the person who just paid real money for a weekend of information has to go apply it entirely on their own.

Information Was Never the Bottleneck

Most people who attend a real estate seminar leave with more information than they'll act on. What they don't leave with is someone to call when they're staring at their first real deal at 9pm on a Tuesday, unsure whether the numbers actually work. That gap — between what you were taught and what actually gets you to close — is the single biggest reason good, motivated people walk away from real estate investing having spent thousands of dollars and closed zero deals.

What FORGE Is Built to Do Differently

FORGE isn't another course, and it isn't a replacement for community or mentorship. It's an AI partner, ARIS, that's available at 9pm on a Tuesday — and every other hour of the week — to run your numbers, answer your question, and help you decide whether a deal is real. It's a deal analyzer that does real underwriting instead of a rough guess. It's a negotiation practice arena so your first hard conversation with a seller isn't also your first time ever having that conversation. The goal was never to argue that live events and coaching don't work. It was to build the thing that was always missing after they ended.

Why We Built It This Way

We spent years watching thousands of people pay for a weekend and walk out with no one to help them apply it. That's not a criticism of any one program — it's a structural limitation of teaching that ends when the event does. An AI advisor doesn't have that limitation. It doesn't get tired, it doesn't need to be scheduled two weeks out, and it remembers every deal you've ever run past it.

Come Judge It Yourself

We'd rather show you than tell you. The clearest way to understand the difference between being taught something once and having a partner who stays with you after is to actually see FORGE and ARIS in action.
