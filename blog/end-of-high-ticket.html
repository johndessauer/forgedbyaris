HOW INVESTOR EDUCATION IS CHANGING — AND WHAT COMES AFTER THE EVENT

October 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Live real estate events and coaching programs still matter. They introduce people to the craft, build belief, and put a clear next step in front of someone who was only curious a week earlier. Our founder, John Dessauer, spent years in that world — on stage and at the back of the room — and FORGE works alongside companies that run those programs. Many of the people we serve first met investing at a seminar or in a coaching room.

So this isn't a call to scrap high-ticket education. Plenty of educators teach honestly, and for the right person a serious coaching package can be the right move. What's changing is the shape of the market around that model: more investors want a way to keep learning and executing after the event, without another five-figure check every time they get stuck.

The Model Is Shifting — Not Disappearing

For a long time, the playbook was simple: free event → paid workshop → large coaching package. That path still exists, and it still works for people who want deep, high-touch mentorship and are ready to invest at that level.

What's under pressure is the idea that one big purchase is the only way to get serious tools and ongoing help. Students leave a strong weekend with notebooks full of ideas, then hit Monday with a real listing on the screen and nobody next to them to ask whether the numbers work. A binder can't answer that question. A weekend can't sit with you every week while you search, underwrite, and chase capital.

That's the gap the next layer of the industry is filling — not instead of good teaching, but beside it.

After the Event: Low Cost, Daily Use, Ongoing Help

FORGE was built for that after layer. It's a monthly membership for an AI operating system that covers coaching, underwriting, negotiation, and capital — serious tools in your pocket, available 24/7, at a fraction of a large upfront package.

Members use it to do the work:

  • Find deals. Property Search surfaces pre-foreclosures, absentee owners, high-equity and vacant properties, and tax-delinquent leads.
  • Analyze them. The Deal Analyzer runs comps for 2–4 units and NOI, cap rate, and DSCR for 5+ units, and it exports a lender-ready PDF.
  • Check the market. The Market Oracle gives a Buy, Watch, or Avoid verdict for any market.
  • Find capital. Capital Radar matches your deal to private lenders actively deploying capital in your target market.
  • Get coached, continuously. The ARIS AI Coach is available 24/7 across wholesaling, flipping, rentals, STR/MTR, multifamily, commercial, and tax, and it remembers your pipeline through the built-in Deal CRM.
  • Keep learning. Education covers 22 topics with three mastery tiers and graded quizzes, and the Real Estate Lab lets you rehearse negotiations against ARIS before the stakes are real.

Pro-level real estate investing tools at a fraction of the cost.

Why a Monthly Layer Keeps Us Honest

A high-touch coaching relationship and a monthly platform solve different jobs. Coaching can be the right intensive path. A monthly membership fits a different need: stay useful on the deals you're looking at this week, so you keep coming back.

That changes the incentive for us. We don't earn membership with one big pitch. We have to earn it again every month by being useful on your actual deals. FORGE isn't for people who want someone else to find and close deals for them, or who are looking for a guaranteed-return investment product. Our 30-day satisfaction guarantee covers the quality of the platform and content (Earnings Disclaimer).

None of this promises income. FORGE is an educational and execution tool, and results depend on your effort and your market. What it offers is a practical next step when the event is over and the real work starts — including for attendees and non-buyers from programs we partner with.

Same Mission. Different Layer.

Good events and coaching open the door. The model around them is evolving so more people can keep going after they walk through it — with search, analysis, capital, and a coach in their pocket while they learn by doing.
