The Origin

ARISTOTLE MENTORED ALEXANDER. ALEXANDER BUILT AN EMPIRE.

In 343 BC, King Philip II of Macedon hired Aristotle — already the most respected mind in the Greek world — to personally tutor his teenage son. For years, Aristotle taught the young prince logic, strategy, ethics, and how to reason through a problem instead of guessing at it.

That student was Alexander. By 30, he had built the largest empire the ancient world had ever seen — not because he inherited it, but because he had a mentor who sharpened how he thought before he ever set foot on a battlefield.

FORGE named its AI coach ARIS for exactly that reason. Most real estate investors don’t fail from a lack of information — there is no shortage of gurus, courses, and hot takes. They fail from a lack of someone sharpening their thinking on this deal, right now. That is the job ARIS was built to do: not to hand you a course, but to mentor the decision in front of you, the way Aristotle mentored the mind that went on to forge an empire.
